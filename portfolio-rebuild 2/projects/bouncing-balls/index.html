Bouncing Balls

Specimen 03 Vanilla Canvas 2D Elastic collisions

Fifteen balls bounce around the canvas, reversing velocity off the walls. When two overlap, both pick a new random color — a simple collision-detect loop with no physics engine, just distance checks between every pair each frame.
