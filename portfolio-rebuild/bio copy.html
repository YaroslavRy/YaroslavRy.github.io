I'm Yaroslav Ryndyk, an AI/ML engineer working at the intersection of neuroscience and machine learning — R&D, neuroscience-inspired AI, and multimodal systems. I'm especially drawn to physically inspired neural networks and how they generalize when paired with simulation environments like Unity, Gazebo, OpenSim, and PyBullet.

My work spans computer vision (including medical imaging), robotics and drones, pose estimation, and signal processing — IMU and EEG / cognitive load analysis. I've also built speech/NLP pipelines: STT, TTS, chatbots, and assistants. Neurotech, cognitive modeling, and next-gen AI systems are what I keep coming back to — this site's specimens (spiking neurons, cellular automata) are basically that interest, made playable.

Currently looking for opportunities in neurotech / BCI / AI roles — feel free to reach out if anything here resonates.
