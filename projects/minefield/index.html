Minefield

Canvas 2D + jQuery 2D convolution

A minesweeper variant: mines are scattered on a grid, and instead of counting each cell's neighbors with nested loops one at a time, the whole board's neighbor counts are computed in one pass with a 2D convolution against a 3×3 kernel. Click a cell to reveal it — hit a mine and the round ends.
