Minefield

Canvas 2D 2D convolution

I was once asked to describe how I would build a Minefield game in JavaScript as a technical task. While thinking through the implementation, I decided to calculate neighboring mine counts with a convolution: represent mines as 1s and empty cells as 0s, then apply a fixed 3×3 kernel. The kernel sums the eight neighbors and skips the cell itself. 

1 1 1
1 0 1
1 1 1

Clear all 170 safe cells on a 14×14 board with 26 mines. Click to reveal; your first click is safe. Numbers count adjacent mines, and empty regions open automatically. Hit a mine and you lose.

Right-click to flag a suspected mine, or enable Flag mode and tap.
