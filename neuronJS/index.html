Model

Leaky integrate-and-fire neurons with excitatory and inhibitory connections. Bright points mark spikes; moving pulses show delayed signals.

Distance: mostly local connections plus sparse long-range links. Random: uniformly sampled targets with immediate delivery.

$$ V_i(t+1) = V_i(t) - V_i(t)/\tau + I_i(t) $$

τ controls how slowly voltage leaks toward zero. At threshold, a neuron spikes, resets to zero, and ignores input during its refractory period. Synaptic strength multiplies each transmitted connection weight.

Time is measured in simulation steps (one per animation frame), not biological milliseconds. Spontaneity is the probability of a voltage kick per neuron per step.

Readouts

Heatmap: recent spikes by position. Raster: spikes from a sampled subset of neurons over time. Phase portrait: population activity versus its change per step.

Parameter sliders affect ongoing activity. Connectivity, neuron count, and degree regenerate the network; Reset starts a fresh random network with the current settings.
